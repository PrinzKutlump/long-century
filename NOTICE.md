# Sources and third-party notices

The [project licence](LICENSE) covers original project materials. The following sources have their own terms.

- **Natural Earth.** Physical and administrative map data are in the public domain. [Terms of use](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/)
- **OpenHistoricalMap contributors.** The geodata workflow uses historical administrative boundaries under CC0 and filters records with incompatible licence tags. [Copyright and licensing](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/OpenHistoricalMap/Copyright)
- **HYDE 3.2.** Klein Goldewijk, K., Beusen, A., Doelman, J. and Stehfest, E. (2017), *Anthropogenic land use estimates for the Holocene – HYDE 3.2*. The DANS deposit is CC0. Population grids are an input to planned map work. [Dataset](https://doi.org/10.17026/dans-25g-gez3)
- **Maddison Project Database, version 2023.** Jutta Bolt and Jan Luiten van Zanden (2024), *Maddison style estimates of the evolution of the world economy: A new 2023 update*, Journal of Economic Surveys. The workbook is distributed unchanged under CC BY 4.0.
  [Article](https://doi.org/10.1111/joes.12618) · [Dataset](https://doi.org/10.34894/INZBF2) · [Licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [Country-series citation instructions](https://www.rug.nl/ggdc/historicaldevelopment/maddison/releases/maddison-project-database-2023)
- **Correlates of War Project.** The calibration reports use derived war counts and summaries of relative national capabilities. Raw source datasets are held privately. Cite the datasets and versions named in the reports. [Data sources and terms](https://correlatesofwar.org/data-sets/)
- **pako.** The game bundles the compression library by Vitaly Puzrin and Andrei Tuputcyn. Its MIT copyright and permission notice is included with the library and embedded in the game build. [Bundled licence](src/vendor/pako-LICENSE.txt)

The [geodata manifest](data/raw/geo/SOURCES.md) records the provenance of the map inputs. The [calibration input inventory](data/raw/README.md) identifies the files included here.
