# The Long Century 1.1 and after

Status: 27 Nov 2026 (Claude, ledger B59). Everything the owner asked for that is not in 1.0 lives here; ledger rows are never dropped. 1.1 work starts only after 1.0 is live, except where a 1.0 ticket needs it. Agents as in `docs/PLAN_1_0.md` section 2.

## 1. Release rhythm after launch

One update every 4 to 6 weeks, each with a devlog post and a short video. Every update keeps crash-free sessions at 99.5% or better and the store rating at 4.3 or better. Updates are built and tested by the same night shift.

| Update | Target | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | May 2027 | Full daily world (DAY5 to DAY7, DAY9), society and interest groups (SOC2), fronts and canals on the map |
| 1.2 | June to July 2027 | Trade and finance: trade page and treaties, taxes by sector, banking and the gold standard, freight by route length |
| 1.3 | August to September 2027 | Navy and industry depth: shipyards and unique ships, naval training, modernisation programmes, more buildings and great works, renovation |
| 1.4 | Autumn 2027 | Online extras (all optional, game stays offline): Ask the Historian, voice orders, cloud saves, player profile and online challenges; intro and event video |
| 1.5 | Late 2027 | The 1917 scenario (Sykes-Picot), the liquid-glass old-book theme, city backgrounds, Steam version |
