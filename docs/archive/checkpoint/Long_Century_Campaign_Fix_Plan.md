# The Long Century | Campaign balance fix plan

The Wider Rollout audit followed eight AI worlds from 1836 to 1936. All reached the end date. The first gameplay priority is giving disputed demands a credible path to settlement before war. The strongest economic warning is how long several goods spend outside their target price range. Both warrant investigation before changing any global difficulty setting.

## Work orders, in implementation order

| Order | Finding | Implementation and acceptance check |
|---|---|---|
| 01 · Establish comparable runs | Century totals show which outcomes missed. They do not identify the decision that caused each miss. | Keep the audited build as the control. In the existing harness record the candidate build hash, seed, year and reason for each crisis, settlement, peace demand, default and construction decision. Add monthly supply, demand and active-use status for flagged goods. Confirm that enabling diagnostics leaves outcomes identical on the same build and seed. |
| 02 · Give diplomacy time to work | There were 45–59 wars against a target of 30–45. Only 1/8 campaigns met the congress target of 5–10 and only 1/8 met the coalition target of 3–8. Of the 21 congresses convened, nine ended peacefully. | Trace ordinary AI disputes and scripted escalations separately. Check whether eligible disputes get a mediation decision before war and whether potential coalition partners have time to respond. Change the most frequent missed opportunity first. Check congresses, coalition formation and war starts together. Retain a peaceful share of at least one-third and report it by seed as well as pooled. |
| 03 · Reduce repeated elimination of states | 14–19 distinct countries lost statehood against a target of 8–15. Average war duration was already in the 12–24 month band on 7/8 seeds. | After order 02, identify remaining eliminations by peace demand, unification, civil war or other event. If ordinary limited disputes regularly end in annexation, make a settlement fulfilling the original demand more attractive and price additional demands by their cost to the victor. Check state losses and duration together. Verify that peace processing preserves legitimate unifications and cannot reverse a settled civil-war result through a later coalition. |
| 04 · Repair persistent commodity imbalances | Wool and rubber each spent 9,544/9,600 observed months outside the 0.60–1.80 price band. Canned goods spent 8,590 months outside it. Cement, fuel, electric goods, fish and tobacco also showed prolonged deviations. | Break down each series by era, production, buyers, traded volume and months since first economic use. Check how the engine prices goods with zero buyers or sellers. Trace missing uses, premature supply, shortages and unprofitable production before changing recipes or investment incentives. Publish both the original all-month metric and the active-market breakdown. Retest the six benchmark goods at all three dates on seeds 7 and 3. Baseline was 34/36 in range, with both misses in clothes in 1900. Target 36/36 and report every remaining century-wide breach separately. |
| 05 · Find what stalls French industry | France reached 84–104.5 factory levels in 1900 against a 100–180 target, passing in 1/8 seeds. No seed met all nine national targets together. The US, Russia and China individually passed in 8/8. | After the price changes, compare French investment proposals, approvals, starts and completions by decade in a passing and failing run. Inspect funds, labour, inputs, queue delays and expected profitability at the first divergence. Correct the evidenced bottleneck, then inspect Britain and Austria, each initially at 6/8. Require all nine national ranges together on at least 6/8 seeds. Preserve the small-country check for nations below six million exceeding 60 factory levels. |
| 06 · Restore credible debt risk | There were 0–2 defaults per century against a target of 2–6. Only 1/8 seeds passed. | Reassess after the war and investment fixes. Trace countries approaching insolvency through interest, debt service, credit limits, recovery and default. If a country can refinance indefinitely or skips debt service, fix that path. If few countries ever borrow, inspect AI borrowing choices first. Count distinct default episodes and verify both a recoverable deficit and a genuine insolvency case. |
| 07 · Verify player decisions across countries | Two scripted seed-7 campaigns survived 1,200 months and generated 100 annual reports each. Britain resolved 14 cards and Punjab resolved two. The audit did not establish how many meaningful actions players could take between cards. | Compare Britain, Punjab, France and Japan across several seeds. Record eligible cards, suppression reasons, actionable orders and the longest stretch without a meaningful choice. Replay one fiscal or construction decision with two alternatives from the same save and compare outcomes. Fix demonstrated gaps in eligibility or feedback. Keep country-specific pacing and avoid duplicate prompts for completed decisions. |

## Validation and release decision

Use seeds 7 and 3 for fast paired comparisons, then run 7, 3, 11, 21, 5, 13, 17 and 29 through January 1936 after each subsystem is accepted. Keep a per-seed before/after table. For century totals where the original plan gives a range without a pass count, use **6/8 as a proposed release minimum** and disclose every miss. Record the worst seed as well as the median. Change one rule family per comparison so regressions can be traced.

Preserve the five required Great Powers in 1900 on all eight seeds. Also check the existing secondary-power requirement for Austria, Italy or Japan on most seeds. Verify German and Italian formation and the Union outcome on at least seven seeds. The baseline passed the core power, formation and Union proxy checks on all eight. Add a direct event-history check for the civil-war settlement because the final-state proxy alone cannot establish how it survived.

Finish with fresh seeds held out of tuning, the existing regression suite, a save/resume comparison and a physical Android portrait playthrough of a crisis, construction order and annual report. Publish any unmet target alongside the candidate build. A full-century run or a passing sample of six commodities cannot establish that every price stayed in range.
