## The Long Century

1836 grand strategy game with monthly turns. Published checkpoint: theatre warfare complete. Military and economy work orders in docs/plans/PLAN.md had already shipped. The latest warfare build uses theatre orders, objectives, intensity, supply and morale, generals, naval stations and missions, and interactive front lines. Economy includes provincial construction, great works, foreign investment, sphere markets, factory modernization, debt and bankruptcy, price and trade charts, and a nations ledger. Settings include the optional £100 million treasury cheat.

## Devlet-i Aliyye

Separate Ottoman game. The original scenario starts January 1911 with monthly selectable decisions and a scored endpoint at the 1918 armistice. The Arab Compact runs December 1917–September 1945 and branches from the Sykes–Picot disclosure, with an Arab loyalty system, interwar reforms, industrial and financial development, research, elections and succession, and Second World War decisions. The shared history records a mobile-first, old-book and liquid-glass design brief, and requirements for legible dashboards, accessible building controls, and decisions with visible consequences.
