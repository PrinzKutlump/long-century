## 3. What worked

- One ticket, one agent, one branch, with CI deciding pass or fail: 21 merges in about two days. ("Main was never broken" is unproven while the century step cannot fail.)
- Release builds on every merge give a playable link without local builds.
- Moving data collection into GitHub Actions produced 1836–1914 history maps that could not be fetched from the sandbox.
- Calibration against COW replaced guessed targets with measured ones (the 30–45 war target was wrong; 49 is real).
