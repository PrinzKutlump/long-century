Source: COW Inter-State War v4.0, Extra-State v4.0, Intra-State v4.1 (source files held privately). Counted by first start year, 1836-1936 inclusive.

| Intra-state (civil) | 139 | 17, 8, 23, 12, 4, 16, 9, 17, 20, 8 |

Findings
- The earlier target of 30-45 wars a century was an estimate. COW's real inter-state count is 49, so the game's current ~50 is on target in total.
- 34 of the 49 involve a great power, the Ottomans or China. A target band of 40-55 inter-state wars, with 25-40 involving a great power, is supported by the data.
- The shape matters more than the total: peaks in the 1860s-70s (unification wars) and the 1910s (Balkan wars, WWI), a trough in the 1880s-90s. Check the game's wars per decade against these.
- Colonial wars (120) and civil wars (139) are far more frequent than inter-state wars; the game should report them as separate rows.
- Source codes use COW country codes.
