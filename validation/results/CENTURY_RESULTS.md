# Century runs

Switzerland was played with default decisions through January 1936. Timings are Node.js wall-clock averages from the final 120 turns and exclude save encoding. Some runs overlapped other tests. These figures do not certify the 130 ms desktop Chromium budget. For seed 3, the less-contended repeat ran at 81.1 ms per month. The original 139.4 ms result is preserved in the raw log.

All four updated payloads are below 1 MB. Four copies of the largest payload require 3,758,536 bytes before browser accounting and metadata. Both repeat runs reproduced their original respective final state digests. Early German unification and excess wars in three seeds remain open.
