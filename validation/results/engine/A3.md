# A3 - one household economy rule for every nation; seat-independent material outcomes; MODEL_REV 3

Base 241d9fa (A2). Result commit: see git log ("A3 ..."). Engine: Node v22.23.5, 2 shared cores.

## What changed
- econ.js: `econMonth` runs the household market (five local goods, employment, household cash, freight cost, tariffs) in every owned province of every living nation. The participant list `["PUN",G.player]` and every `hasProvincialEconomy` branch (econ.js x4, society.js x4, the function itself) are gone. landUse and employerAccount are written for all provinces.
- Measured cause and the one model change (see section 3): the Punjab-pilot import cap in `allocateLocalHouseholds` is replaced by "the nation's pool is delivered up to the province's demand; freight cost and tariffs still apply; occupied provinces receive nothing".
- events.js/diplo.js/build.js/regional.js: every seat exclusion that changed a material outcome was removed. Human decision points use one mechanism: `humanSeat(tag)` (control flag: G.player and not ai), `askHuman` yes/no card (kind "dec", default "yes" = the AI result), `pullIn` (war invitations), `toSphere`, `toAnnex`, plebiscite, sphere annexation, Company/Lahore offer. italy3 and germany4 now fire for every seat; a human SAR/AUS/PRU gets the renewal as a card (renew or wait; AUS fight or cede / fight or yield), AI default renews, identical result (test_a3.js).
- ui.js: `employerAccount` is not saved; `localMarket`/`landUse` are saved only for the played nation's provinces (otherwise the save grows by the per-province rows of 59 nations).
- test_observer.js: A3_DONE=true, window 120 months, nobody/PUN/GBR/SWI identical for economy and full material digests, plus market-row coverage and finiteness. tools/a3_century.js: 100-year lockstep (nobody/AUS/PRU/SAR/FRA seats, seed 7, digests every 12 months) identical; no NaN/Infinity in the whole state at any year.
- MODEL_REV 3. test_d4.js: province id taken once (a congress can hand DEN's only province over; the shifted RNG exposed it).

## 3. Measured cause of the first failure (before any number was changed)
First version: every province used the pilot's import cap (3% of farm capacity + 4% per rail level + 20 per depot). Result (a3-capped-seed-*.jsonl): grain 0.21-0.46 on all 8 seeds (target 0.6), seed 7 world population 1,961k (A2) to 1,044k in 1936, living standards 0.99 to 0.80. tools/a3_probe.js and a diagnostic of one year: 105 of 161 provinces short of grain, deficit 465k units against an import capacity of 13.9k (3%), pop-weighted grain fill 56%, timber 11%, livestock 15%; low fill drives `ld` down and population growth negative. The same cap already shrinks Punjab itself (11k to 1k pop by 1866 under A2) and, until now, only the player's nation. Variants (seed 7, 100 years): cap removed: world pop 1,887k, grain 0.64; cap x20: 1,819k, grain 0.68. Chosen: no cap inside controlled territory (structural, no new constant); B1 should reintroduce capacity from the freight graph. Consequence for B3: Punjab no longer has a rail-capacity grain bottleneck until B1.
