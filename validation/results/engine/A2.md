# A2 - one funding rule for every payer; common project transitions; AI obeys facCan; MODEL_REV 2

Base f2e410b (A1). Result commit: see git log ("A2 ...").

## What changed
- `investMonth`: every payer (public tr, private inv pool, foreign sponsor inv) pays at most its balance: fraction = min(1, max(0,balance)/bill), whether or not a ledger exists. The ledger (full, player-only) is now written after payment and cannot change funding. No compact AI ledger was needed: `b.paid` and the terminal record carry what reporting needs.
- Common transitions in econ.js: `newPid` (every project has a stable `pid`; queue promotion keeps `<tag>/<queue id>`), `projectPayer`, `endProject` (single exit: completed or canceled, records spent / recoverable 0 / unspent), `completeProject`, `queueEnd` (dropped or removed queue order). `transferProv` ends every project through `endProject` (existing cancel-without-refund policy retained); an extinct owner's queue is cleared.
- AI factory waiver removed (AI_LEGACY_FAC and facCan's legacy flag deleted). MODEL_REV = 2.
- Event data (shared mechanism, no nation-specific core code): see section 3.

## 3. Why Italy (and Germany, and the Union) failed, measured
A1 reported "enforcing facCan made Italy never form on seed 7". The causes, measured with tools/a2_italy_probe.js on four rule variants (head = waiver, no limit; b = facCan only; c = funding limit only; a2 = both), seeds 3/7/11/21 (a2-italy-1859-variants.txt):
- The italy1 conditions are met in every run (SAR alive, AUS owns LOM, no war, civ, truces over) and the war is declared in January 1859 in every run. Sardinia's own treasury (16k-70k), army (7-24 brigades) and factories (0.5-10.5 levels) do not predict the result; France joins in all 16 runs.
- SAR+FRA win 12 of 16 runs and lose 4 (head seed 3, b seed 7, c seed 21, a2 seed 21): one loss per variant, so neither facCan nor the funding limit moves the odds measurably. The loss on seed 7 in A1 was one draw of a ~25% event, made visible because any rule change reshuffles the random stream.
- The real defect: italy1 is a one-shot. A lost war leaves `G.unif.ITA` unset and nothing renews it, so Italy cannot form for the rest of the century (f2e410b already had this on seed 3). germany2 has the same gap: PRU lost it on seeds 7 and 21 (war score -32, -28) and Germany never formed.
- Fix, event data only: `italy3` (1866) and `germany4` (1868) renew the same war once, if it was lost, both sides are at peace, the truce is over, and neither is a human seat; germany4 keeps the 0.8 land-power gate with a later fallback year (R8). They did not fire on seeds 3 and 11 (hashes there are from rule changes alone).
- Union: seed 3 lost the civil war after A2's funding change. Measured: the secession (Jan 1861) came in the middle of a great-power war, with 44 conscript brigades raised; the event split the whole army by population share, so the Confederacy got 26-32 brigades (usual 10) that nothing would ever demobilise, and the Union then demobilised its conscripts (Confederate 26-32 against Union 19-47, war score -51; 8 other runs: CSA 4-12, Union won every one, ws 60-80). Fix, event data: the split uses the standing army only (`!u.mob`); conscripts stay with the Union's mobilisation plan. Seed 3 then restores the Union in December 1861 (a first attempt, delaying secession until peace, still lost and was dropped).
No number was tuned.
